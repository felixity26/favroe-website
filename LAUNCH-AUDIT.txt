DECISION
Not ready for live payments. A sandbox payment succeeds, but that does not prove inventory safety, fulfillment, webhook persistence, or delivery of notifications. This candidate deliberately blocks live checkout until atomic inventory allocation is implemented and verified. It does not change the approved branding, sleeve renderings, prices, or live payment configuration.

IMPLEMENTED IN THIS CANDIDATE, NOT YET DEPLOYED
- Checkout validation and price checks precede stock writes. Inventory failures stop session creation instead of allowing checkout through.
- One-hour reservation expiration, stable on same-key retries. Separate sandbox inventory ledger; refunds never replenish either ledger.
- 400-set frontend cap, bulk quote link, explicit $12 shipping copy, corrected test-mode text and accessible shipping-state label.
- Webhook verifies latest Stripe session, configured price IDs, quantities, brand and payment mode. Supports completed and async-payment-succeeded events; handles signature rotation and invalid timestamps.
- Paid orders retain paid status while unsupported/missing shipping addresses are held. Every sandbox order has a test hold. A hold is NOT a substitute for preventing unsupported-address payment before launch.
- Customer and operations paid-order notifications via Resend, durable sent markers and provider idempotency keys. Provider failure leaves webhook retryable after saving the paid order. Provider acceptance is NOT inbox delivery verification.
- Partial and full refunds distinguished; neither restocks. Payment return explicitly identifies sandbox transactions and refunds.
- Inquiry sanitation preserves hyphens in names, email, dates, ZIP+4 and idempotency keys; rejects invalid quantity rather than silently clamping it; limits payload size and checks Origin.
- Operations writes use record storage keys. Inquiry statuses cannot fabricate payment/refund records; sandbox/held/unpaid orders cannot be released to fulfillment.
- Five drafted policy pages and static /links/ page included. False inquiry “we emailed you” promise removed until delivery is known. Removed catalog-draft links from changed pages.
- All original public/assets files are byte-for-byte unchanged. Sleeve visuals retained; sleeve exclusion clarified beside product imagery and purchase choices.

LAUNCH BLOCKERS / REMAINING WORK
1. ATOMIC INVENTORY: Current KV read-then-write reservations and event markers are eventually consistent. Concurrent checkouts can oversell; duplicate webhook deliveries can race. Replace authoritative allocation/order/event writes with a transactional D1 design or Durable Object, migrate/reconcile records and test concurrent last-stock purchases. This candidate keeps live checkout blocked meanwhile.
2. FINAL ADDRESS: The website's pre-check is insufficient once Stripe allows address edits. The new webhook hold prevents fulfillment but may occur after payment. Implement and verify pre-payment final-address restrictions or an authorized payment/hold workflow before launch.
3. DEPLOYMENT VERIFICATION: No Cloudflare dashboard login/deploy token is available in this session. GitHub token authenticates GitHub, not Cloudflare. Confirm the dashboard deployment commit, bindings, secret NAMES, webhook event subscriptions and preview deployment. Do not overwrite live with the old Netlify ZIP.
4. EMAIL: Verify a sender domain for RESEND_FROM; committed onboarding@resend.dev is a test sender. Confirm customer and essentials@favroe.com inbox delivery, bounced mail handling and webhook notification retries. Inquiry email failures are still best-effort; add a retryable inquiry outbox before calling sales capture dependable. Instinct's operating destination remains unconnected.
5. PERSISTENCE: Inspect the actual test order and inquiry in ops, reconcile totals, verify 50/100/200/400 purchases reduce test inventory exactly once, expire abandoned reservations, replay duplicate webhooks and refund without restock. These checks require authenticated ops/Stripe/Cloudflare access and are unverified on the deployed candidate.
6. STRIPE IDENTITY: Correct checkout business display name from Forvae to approved FAVRŒ; verify allowed payment methods. Existing Stripe payment-method configuration is preserved; delayed methods still require end-to-end verification.
7. SELLER PERMIT/TAX: Live tax remains blocked with live payments. Sandbox automatic tax is enabled. Verify permit, registration and approved tax settings before separate live authorization.
8. INQUIRIES: Add rate limiting and transactional idempotency/outbox. Existing content deduplication uses time buckets and can race; do not treat it as exactly-once processing.
9. POLICIES: Verify privacy notice against actual deployed processing/retention/providers and final approved operational commitments. Pages supplied are implementation drafts, not a legal compliance certification.
10. SOCIAL/SEO: Actual social profile URLs are still pending; do not invent them. Keep noindex while testing. After checkout/operations acceptance, separately review production indexing, canonical URLs, sitemap, schema and link previews.

MERGE / DEPLOY ORDER
Review this candidate as a draft. Preserve all Cloudflare secret values and existing KV bindings. Deploy to a test preview first, configure a separate test webhook, and rerun E2E. Paid events must remain unprocessed/retryable if durable order or notification work fails. Do not activate live keys, remove the live guard, or publish a claim that the site is ready for commercial orders until the blockers are resolved and the founder authorizes launch.
