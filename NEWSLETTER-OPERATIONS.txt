Flow:
POST /api/newsletter/signup validates email, explicit consent version, same-origin JSON and a honeypot, and enforces bounded-body and rate limits. Signup sends one confirmation email, never a campaign. Confirmation tokens are random, stored only as hashes, expire after 24h and require an explicit confirmation POST. Email scanners following GET cannot subscribe people.
/newsletter/confirm and /newsletter/unsubscribe keep tokens in URL fragments, remove them from the address bar, and use no-referrer. Confirmation activates only the FAVRŒ notes topic. Global opt-outs are never silently reset. Unsubscribe also invalidates old confirmation links. The separate newsletter notice describes data handling without changing existing policies.

Rate controls:
8 signup attempts per connection/hour, one confirmation request per normalized email/day, and at most 30 confirmation sends/day. These limits are deliberately modest to reserve capacity for orders and reduce abuse. They are not a guarantee of remaining provider allowance; Resend failures are shown as errors. No automatic paid upgrade.
Failed sends are not claimed successful. Transient confirmation failures remain retryable. Unconfirmed requests are removed after 30 days during signup processing; confirmed consent is retained for support. Preferences work independently from order mail.
