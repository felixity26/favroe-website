What we record

We store your email address, the signup page, the consent wording you saw, and the times you requested and confirmed signup. We also record unsubscribe activity. Cloudflare hosts this separate newsletter record; Resend handles confirmation emails, subscriber preferences and newsletters.

We activate signup only after you confirm your email. Confirmation links expire after 24 hours. Unconfirmed requests are removed after 30 days during routine signup processing. Confirmed consent records are retained to manage your preferences and respond to questions; contact us if you would like to request deletion.

Changing your mind

Use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter or email essentials@favroe.com. Unsubscribing from the newsletter does not stop necessary order or inquiry messages.

We use short-lived counters derived from technical connection information to reduce signup abuse. The newsletter database does not store your raw IP address. See our privacy policy for information about the wider website and your requests.
