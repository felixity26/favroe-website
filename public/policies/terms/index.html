The practical details

Terms of sale.

A clear understanding, from the start.

What you are buying

Each complete set includes one aluminum food container, one fitted lid, and one individually wrapped pink shovel spoon. Packaging only. Food and sealing stickers are not included. The branded FAVRŒ cover is a separate branding option and is never included in the standard set price.

Available finishes

Pink is the available launch finish. Aluminum is shown for preview only and cannot be purchased. Images are product renderings; actual-stock photography is pending. Branded covers shown in presentation imagery are separate options.

Prices and quantities

Prices are in US dollars: Discovery 50 — $19.99; Starter 100 — $34.99; Wholesale 200 — $64.99; Volume 400 — $119.99. Wholesale starts at 200 sets. Introductory quantities of 50 and 100 remain available. A maximum of 400 complete sets can be purchased in one online order, including mixed packs.

Shipping and tax

Online shipping is $12 per order to the lower 48 states only. Shipping and any applicable tax are shown before payment. The website is currently in test mode. No live sales or live tax collection are active. Once the seller’s permit and live checkout are approved, California sales tax will be calculated at checkout for California deliveries only. No sales tax will be collected by FAVRŒ on deliveries to other states under the launch configuration.

Payment and confirmation

Payment is handled through Stripe. Starting checkout or requesting a quote does not confirm a paid order. A paid order requires successful payment verification. Test transactions are not commercial orders and will not be fulfilled.

Larger quantities and branding

For more than 400 sets or a branded cover, request a quote. Scope, availability, pricing and timing must be agreed before payment. No customization price is included in the standard set prices.

Dispatch and delivery

Orders ship within 3 business days. Wholesale orders are delivered in 7–10 business days. Read our shipping policy for destinations and delay handling.

Final sale and damage

All sales are final; no returns are accepted. Damage is handled by replacement only when reported to essentials@favroe.com within 5 days of delivery with photos of the box and damaged items. See our returns and replacement policy. These policies do not limit any rights or remedies that applicable law requires us to provide.

Product use

Container dimensions are 166 × 65 × 30 mm, with an approximate capacity of 200 mL. The spoon is 120 × 30 mm and individually wrapped. Lid and wrapper dimensions and material-use specifications remain subject to supplier confirmation. Do not assume suitability for a heating method or food application that has not been confirmed.

Contact

The seller is FAVRŒ. For order questions or support, email essentials@favroe.com.
