Privacy.

Current position

This local prototype collects test business/contact details only when you choose to submit a test inquiry. The data stays in the local server’s memory, is visible in the local review inbox, and is deleted on server restart or after 24 hours. No email delivery, payment processing, analytics or third-party tracking is connected. The form does not store submitted details in browser storage. The shopping bag stores product IDs and quantities in this browser; it stores no contact or payment information.

Before launch

Before public use: confirm the operating entity and contact, data processors, purpose, retention, access controls, deletion/request procedures and applicable requirements. Do not enter real customer information into this test flow.
