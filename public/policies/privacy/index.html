The practical details

Privacy.

Your details deserve consideration, too.

Information you share

When you inquire or order, we collect the details needed to help: your name, business name, email, optional phone number, business location, delivery address or ZIP code, requested quantities and dates, product requirements, and messages you send us. Order records also include items, quantities, payment references, amounts and fulfillment status.

Why we use it

We use these details to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, process orders, arrange delivery, provide support and replacements, prevent misuse, and maintain business records. A quote request is kept separate from a paid order.

Payments

Stripe handles the payment form. Do not send card details by email or through our inquiry form. FAVRŒ’s order system receives payment references and status information, not your full card number or card security code. Stripe explains its handling of payment information in its privacy policy.

Who helps us provide the service

Information is processed by providers supporting website hosting, payments, email delivery, storage and shipping, and by authorized people or operational assistants supporting FAVRŒ. We share the information needed for the relevant task. Our current website uses Cloudflare for hosting and Stripe for checkout.

Browser storage and technical information

The shopping bag uses browser storage to remember your selections. You can clear it through your browser settings. Hosting and payment services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and security events. Browser Do Not Track signals do not change the essential processing needed to handle an inquiry or order.

Your requests

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have shared with FAVRŒ, email essentials@favroe.com. We may need to verify the request and retain records required for accounting, legal obligations or resolving an order issue.

Keeping this notice current

We will update this page when our practices change and show the revision date below. Questions about this notice can be sent to essentials@favroe.com.
