The practical details

Shipping & delivery.

A little clarity before your next batch.

Where we ship

We ship from Riverside, California, to the contiguous United States: the lower 48 states. We do not ship to Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, or international destinations. Local pickup is not offered through checkout.

One order. One shipping charge.

Shipping is $12 per online order, shown before payment. The online order limit is 400 complete sets. For larger quantities, request a bulk quote; the final delivery arrangements and total will be confirmed before payment.

When to expect it

Orders ship within 3 business days. Wholesale orders are delivered in 7–10 business days. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

Check your delivery details

Please check your shipping address before paying. If something needs correcting, email essentials@favroe.com. We will review what can be changed before dispatch. Orders with an address outside our shipping area cannot be released for shipment.

If dispatch is delayed

If we cannot ship within the promised time, we will contact you with a revised shipping date and the option to cancel for a refund, as required by applicable law.

Tax

The website is currently in test mode. No live sales or live tax collection are active. Once the seller’s permit and live checkout are approved, California sales tax will be calculated at checkout for California deliveries only. No sales tax will be collected by FAVRŒ on deliveries to other states under the launch configuration.

Something arrived damaged?

Email essentials@favroe.com within 5 days of delivery with photos of the shipping box and damaged items. Our damage policy provides a replacement, not a refund. Read the returns and damage policy.
