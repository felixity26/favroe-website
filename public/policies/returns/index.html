Please choose your quantity carefully. We do not accept returns. All sales are final.

If your order arrives damaged

We want the practical things to arrive as they should. Email essentials@favroe.com within 5 days of delivery. Include your order reference and clear photos of the shipping box and the damaged items.

A replacement for the damaged items

We provide replacements for damage reported within that window with the requested photos. We do not offer refunds for damage. Please contact us before sending any items back; reporting damage does not authorize a return.
