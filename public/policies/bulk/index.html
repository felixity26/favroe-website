The practical details

Wholesale & bulk orders.

A larger plan starts with a conversation.

Wholesale starts at 200 sets

Wholesale 200 is $64.99. Volume 400 is $119.99. Introductory quantities are also available: Discovery 50 for $19.99 and Starter 100 for $34.99. All prices are in US dollars.

What is included

Each complete set includes one aluminum food container, one fitted lid, and one individually wrapped pink shovel spoon. Packaging only. Food and sealing stickers are not included. The branded FAVRŒ cover is a separate branding option and is never included in the standard set price.

Up to 400 online. More by quote.

The online order limit is 400 complete sets across all selected packs. For larger quantities, send a bulk inquiry with your business details, required quantity, delivery ZIP code and preferred date. Submitting the form starts a conversation; it does not create a paid order or reserve stock.

A cover of your own

Branded covers are separate options. Design scope, quantities, price and production timing must be confirmed in a written quote. The standard set prices do not include a cover or other customization.

Shipping and timing

Online orders have a $12 shipping charge and ship to the lower 48 states only. Orders ship within 3 business days; wholesale orders are delivered in 7–10 business days. For quantities above the online limit or custom branding, confirm the full delivery arrangements and total before paying.

Tax

The website is currently in test mode. No live sales or live tax collection are active. Once the seller’s permit and live checkout are approved, California sales tax will be calculated at checkout for California deliveries only. No sales tax will be collected by FAVRŒ on deliveries to other states under the launch configuration.

Final sale and damage

All sales are final. We do not accept returns. For damaged items, email essentials@favroe.com within 5 days of delivery with photos of the shipping box and the damaged items. We provide replacements, not damage refunds. These policies do not limit any rights or remedies that applicable law requires us to provide.

Keep the details together

Your written quote and verified payment reference should stay with your order. For updates, questions or support, email essentials@favroe.com.
