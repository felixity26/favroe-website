A few useful answers

Before you ask.

Is 200 the minimum for every purchase?

No. The wholesale minimum is 200 complete sets. Discovery 50 and Starter 100 are introductory options below that minimum.

What does a complete set include?

One aluminum container, one fitted lid and one individually wrapped pink mini shovel spoon. Food and sealing stickers are not included.

Are you selling cakes or prepared food?

We supply food packaging and presentation accessories. You prepare the food; our sets provide the container, lid and spoon.

How much is delivery?

Shipping or delivery is charged separately. The rate depends on the packed order and destination and is confirmed before an order. There is no published flat rate.

Can I request more than 400 sets?

Yes. Submit the quantity and destination for an individual quotation. Availability, supplier lead times and terms must be confirmed before an order is accepted.
